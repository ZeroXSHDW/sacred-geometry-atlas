    Notes on the atlas model

    01 — 04

    Every church is an argument between the line and the circle.

    This atlas translates a building into a small vocabulary of measurable relationships: axes, modules, radii, envelopes, and proportions. Switch between outside and inside to see how the structural shell becomes a spatial sequence.

    A

    Axis

    The dominant directional line. It can be longitudinal, radial, or crossed by a transept.

    A = {p₁ → p₂}
    B

    Module

    A repeatable unit that sets the pace of bays, columns, windows, and structural spans.

    M = L ÷ n
    C

    Envelope

    The enclosing figure: rectangle, cross, ellipse, polygon, or a compound of simple forms.

    E = {x, y, r, θ}
    D

    Symmetry

    A reading of how closely the plan mirrors itself across its principal axes.

    S = 1 − (Δ ÷ P)
    i

    Important: the current collection is a schematic proportional study, not a measured survey or a claim about every church. Replace the values in data/geometry.js with your own measured dataset to expand the atlas.
