    01 — 04

    Every church is an argument between the line and the circle.

    This atlas translates a building into a small vocabulary of geometric relationships: axes, modules, radii, envelopes, and proportions. Switch between outside and inside to see how the structural shell becomes a spatial sequence.

    A

    Axis

    The dominant directional line. It can be longitudinal, radial, or crossed by a transept.

    A = {p₁ → p₂}
    B

    Module

    A repeatable unit that sets the pace of bays, columns, windows, and structural spans.

    M = L ÷ n
    C

    Envelope

    The enclosing figure: rectangle, cross, ellipse, polygon, or a compound of simple forms.

    E = {x, y, r, θ}
    D

    Symmetry

    A reading of how closely the plan mirrors itself across its principal axes.

    S = 1 − (Δ ÷ P)

    Research key

    How to read the derived values

    These readings describe the proportional model; they are analytical aids, not measured survey results.

    Floor area reading
    Uses the supplied floor-area estimate when available; otherwise it displays a bounding area from length × span. The active reading labels which basis applies.
    Section ratio
    Height ÷ span. A larger value indicates a taller section relative to its widest enclosing dimension.
    Module / span
    Notional bay module ÷ span. It shows how much of the cross-width one repeated unit occupies.
    Radial reach
    The primary radius recorded for the study’s central, apsidal, oval, or other curved figure.
    Estimated volume
    An optional estimate. Its calculation basis and provenance are shown beside the value; missing estimates remain explicitly marked.
    Reading profile
    Four interpretive 0–100 scores. Linearity and verticality follow normalized length ÷ span and height ÷ span; radiality follows a typology cue; repetition follows normalized bay count. They describe proportional tendencies, not empirical measurements.

    Dataset contract

    What each record contains

    JSON records and CSV rows describe the same study objects, while the published schema makes the contract reusable. Dimensions follow the schema unit system; optional estimates stay blank when a source does not supply them.

    Open JSON ↗ Open CSV ↗ Open schema ↗
    Identity
    id, index, name, and shortName identify a stable study and its display labels.
    Context
    typology, place, era, churchName, emphasis, and axis situate the reading.
    Envelope
    length, span, height, envelope, and type describe the enclosing figure.
    Rhythm
    bayCount, module, and details record repetition, pace, and named geometric components.
    Curvature & balance
    radius records the primary curved reach; symmetry is a normalized 0–1 reading.
    Interpretation
    surfaceNote, exteriorNote, and interiorNote keep the visual reading attached to each record.
    Provenance
    status, source, sourceUrl, and sourceNote state the record’s data status, named-building reference, and provenance.
    Optional estimates
    floorAreaEstimate, volumeEstimate, and volumeBasis are included only when supplied by the study; derived exports also identify the floor-area basis.
