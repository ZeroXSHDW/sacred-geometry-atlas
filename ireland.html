03 / research ledger

Use authoritative map sources before commercial use.

Source-linked · rights-aware · updateable

A

Tailte Éireann boundary data

Official boundary data includes counties, provinces, townlands, administrative areas, and other boundary families. The official guidance says boundary maps are not legal property boundaries and recommends due diligence where precision matters.

Read boundary guidance ↗
B

GeoHive / public geospatial hub

GeoHive is the Irish state geospatial hub for authoritative government and Tailte Éireann information. It is the right next source when this schematic studio is upgraded to a data-backed map layer.

Explore GeoHive ↗
C

Open-data licence check

Source geometry, labels, historic maps, symbols, and photography can have different terms. Keep a source URL and licence record beside every production asset; do not assume that a publicly visible map is automatically cleared for resale.

Browse Ireland’s open data portal ↗
