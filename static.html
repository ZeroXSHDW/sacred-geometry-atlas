Reading key

How to read the geometry records

Each record’s data status and provenance note describe its evidence level; the values below are analytical aids and should not be read as measured survey results without supporting source context.

Axis
The dominant directional line through the plan or section.
Length ÷ span
A higher ratio reinforces a long, processional reading.
Height ÷ span
A taller value indicates stronger vertical emphasis.
Module
The notional repeat unit that sets the pace of bays or frames.
Symmetry index
A normalized 0–1 reading of how closely the plan mirrors itself.
Floor area reading
Uses a supplied floor-area estimate when available; otherwise it uses length × span. Each record names the basis.
Estimated volume
An optional estimate; its calculation basis and provenance travel with the record.
Reading profile
Four interpretive 0–100 scores: normalized length ÷ span, height ÷ span, a typology cue, and normalized bay count. They describe tendencies, not empirical measurements.
